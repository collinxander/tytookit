Who I Am

A film photographer
& educator.

My photography is dedicated to capturing the raw, unfiltered essence of Queer, Trans, Black, Indigenous, and People of Color (QTBIPOC), as well as the quiet, overlooked moments in nature and human connection. The intentionality required by film photography — the patience, the vintage gear, and the unpredictability of every frame — has taught me grace, resilience, and a deeper connection to my community and ancestors.

Whether I am behind the camera or helping others discover their own creative spark, my goal is simply to share the beauty that surrounds us and create a space where we can all be seen.

I'm deeply grateful in advance for those I have yet to co-create with. Let's make something timeless.
